Dish descriptions

Biryani
Fragrant rice cooked with spices, vegetables or marinated meat.
Masala Dosa
A crisp rice-and-lentil crepe filled with spiced potatoes.
Gulab Jamun
Soft milk-solid dumplings soaked in sweet cardamom syrup.
