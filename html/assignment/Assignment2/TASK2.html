--College Terminology--

Semester
A half-year academic term in which classes and exams are conducted.
Attendance
The percentage of classes a student attends during a course.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or technical work.
Faculty
Teachers or academic staff responsible for teaching and guidance.
Dean
The head of a faculty or academic department.
Placement
Process of getting students jobs in companies after completing studies.
