Welcome to Chitkara University

Chitkara University stands out as one of the best private universities near Chandigarh, Punjab, India. Established in 2002 by the Chitkara Educational Trust (CET), and founded in 1997 by dedicated educators Dr. Ashok K. Chitkara and Dr. Madhu Chitkara , it rests on pillars of academic excellence, innovation, and integrity.
UGC-recognised and accredited with NAAC A+, the university offers top undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral program options across Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Health Sciences, Nursing, Hospitality, Law, Art & Design, and Education. Its curriculum is regularly updated with industry input to ensure relevance, practical exposure, and strong employment outcomes.
Chitkara University is consistently ranked among the best by leading national and global bodies such as NIRF, THE World University Rankings, QS World University Rankings, and others. Home to over 30,000 students from 28 countries and supported by more than 900 faculty members, the university fosters a diverse, inclusive, and academically rigorous environment.
Chitkara University is known for multidisciplinary, industry-aligned education, supported by collaborations with 300+ leading universities and colleges worldwide. With a strong focus on research, innovation, experiential learning, and student support, the university prepares graduates with the skills, confidence, and global perspective needed for meaningful careers and responsible leadership.
